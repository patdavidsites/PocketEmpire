Cosy Empires

Gather materials, build an empire... and beware the night. Matches run about 10–15 minutes.

  • Drag to pan · pinch or scroll to zoom · Apple Pencil drags a selection box directly
  • On a computer: ↑ / ↓ zoom, WASD or ← / → pan, . jumps to an idle villager
  • Tap a unit to select · press and hold a moment, then drag, to box-select
  • With units selected, tap Gather, Build, Attack or Move in the bar below, then tap the target
  • Villagers who build a Farm, Mine or Lumber Camp stay on as its crew — three each
  • Knowledge accrues on its own — build Elder's Lodges to speed it up. It gates your ages.
  • Win by destroying every enemy building
